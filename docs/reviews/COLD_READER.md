# Review brief: cold reader

**Audience:** an agent reviewing a draft. This is a prompt, so read it as your
instructions.

This brief tests the top of a document, which is where every problem this
repository had was concentrated. It applies to `README.md`, the site landing
page, and anything else a stranger meets first.

## The constraint that makes this work

**Read only the first screen.** That means roughly the first forty lines of
markdown, or everything above the first fold of the rendered page. Stop there.

Do not read the rest of the document. Do not read any other file in the
repository. Do not follow a link. If you already know this project from earlier
context, say so in your report, because it compromises the test and the person
reading your answer needs to know.

The value here comes entirely from answering with less information than you
would like. Guessing is the point.

## What to report

Answer in this order, before any analysis.

1. **What is this?** One sentence, in your own words.
2. **Who is it for?** One sentence.
3. **What has it achieved?** List what you believe the author actually built or
   found. If you cannot tell, say that.
4. **How does the author come across?** One sentence. Be blunt.
5. **Would you keep reading?** Yes or no, and why.
6. **Would you star it?** Yes or no, and why.

Then, and only then:

7. **What did you have to guess at**, and what would have removed the guess?
8. **What was the first thing you read**, quoted exactly, and what did it make
   you expect?
9. **Was anything above the fold spent on something that could have waited?**
   Quote it and say where it belongs.

## Calibration

A pass looks like this. Questions 1 and 2 are answered correctly and
confidently. Question 3 names something concrete. Question 4 is positive without
your having been told to be positive. Questions 5 and 6 are yes.

A failure looks like this: hedged answers to 1 and 2, "I cannot tell" on 3, or a
question 8 quote that turns out to be a caveat, a correction, or an apology.
Every one of those was the real state of this repository's front page.

Report what you actually thought. A polite answer here is worth nothing, and an
inaccurate one is worse, because the whole point is to find out what a stranger
takes away before anyone explains anything to them.

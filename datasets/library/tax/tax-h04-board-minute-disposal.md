# Minutes of a meeting of the board of directors
Larkin Fabrication Ltd, first meeting of the year

Present: three directors. Apologies: one.

1. Warehouse lease. The board considered the renewal of the warehouse lease,
which expires at the end of the third quarter. The landlord has offered a five-year term at
$164,000 per annum with a tenant break at year three. The board resolved to
instruct the property advisers to negotiate, with authority up to $172,000 per
annum.

2. Insurance renewal. The board noted that the combined liability policy renews
at the start of the new policy year and that the broker has been instructed to market it. The board
resolved that cover must not be allowed to lapse and that the finance director
shall confirm binding not less than one week before renewal.

3. Plant replacement. The board reviewed the replacement schedule for the press
line. Expenditure of $310,000 was approved in principle, subject to a further
paper at the March meeting.

4. Any other business. None.

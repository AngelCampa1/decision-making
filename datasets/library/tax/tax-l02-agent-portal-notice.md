# Agent portal - scheduled maintenance
Meridian Revenue Authority, Digital Services. Notice DS/2211.

The agent portal will be unavailable for 36 hours of scheduled maintenance. The
work affects the submission channel and the correspondence archive equally, and
agents should plan on the basis that neither is reachable while it runs.

Filings that would ordinarily fall in the window may be made before or after it.
The Authority has confirmed that the interruption is not treated as affecting
any filing position. Agents holding items in draft should note their content
locally: drafts held in the portal for more than 90 days are not guaranteed to
survive the upgrade, and roughly 1,200 such drafts are currently held.

The helpdesk continues on its usual hours, 08:00 to 18:00, throughout. Queries
about the maintenance go to Digital Services on extension 4402 rather than to
the Assessments Branch, which has no visibility of the portal schedule.

Issued for information. No response required.

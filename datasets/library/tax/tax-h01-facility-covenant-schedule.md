# Facility agreement - schedule of financial covenants
Meridian Commercial Bank, facility dated in the prior financial year

Clause 18.1 The Borrower shall maintain a ratio of consolidated net borrowings
to consolidated EBITDA of not more than 3.25:1, tested semi-annually at each half-year end.

Clause 18.2 The Borrower shall deliver to the Agent, within 45 days of each
testing date, a compliance certificate signed by two directors confirming the
ratio and setting out its calculation in reasonable detail.

Clause 18.4 The Borrower must notify the Agent in writing within 5 Business Days
of becoming aware of any event which would, with the giving of notice or the
lapse of time, constitute an Event of Default under this Agreement.

Clause 18.9 The financial covenants in this Schedule apply only to the Borrower
and its Subsidiaries as constituted at the date of this Agreement, and no
undertaking in this Schedule extends to any tax or regulatory matter, which is
dealt with exclusively under Clause 22.

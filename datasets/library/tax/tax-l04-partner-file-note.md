# File note - annual client review
Halloran & Reese, partner review. Review cycle 12.

The annual review of the client relationship was carried out. The client remains
within the firm's ordinary risk appetite, scoring 2 on the internal 5-point
scale, and no change to engagement terms is proposed. Fees of $46,500 for the
cycle were discussed and the client indicated satisfaction with the arrangement.

The client expects activity levels to be broadly flat, with turnover projected
within 4% of the prior cycle, and no significant transactions are contemplated.
On that basis the compliance timetable agreed at the outset remains appropriate
and has not been revised.

Two administrative points were noted. The registered correspondence address is
out of date following a move 7 months ago, and the authorised signatory list
carries 1 name who has since left. Both have gone to the administration team.

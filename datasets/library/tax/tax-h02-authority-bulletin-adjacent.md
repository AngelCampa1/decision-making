# Authority Bulletin on the deposit return scheme - deposit return scheme
Meridian Revenue Authority

1. This Bulletin sets out the Authority's practice in relation to the deposit
return scheme introduced with effect from the start of the current scheme year. It has no application
to any other levy or charge.

2. A scheme participant must register within 30 days of first handling a
scheme container. Registration is made under reg. 4 and takes effect from the
date of the application, not from the date of acceptance.

3. Where a participant fails to register within the period in paragraph 2, the
Authority may impose a fixed charge of $2,750 and a further charge of $110 for
each complete month of continued non-registration.

4. Nothing in this Bulletin affects the operation of the assessment or
reassessment provisions, which are outside its scope. Participants with
questions about those provisions should refer to the relevant published guidance
rather than to this Bulletin.

# Combined liability policy - schedule of cover
Meridian Mutual, policy period commencing at the last renewal

Section A - Employers liability. Limit of indemnity $10,000,000 any one
occurrence. The Insured must notify the Insurer in writing within 21 days of
becoming aware of any circumstance likely to give rise to a claim.

Section C - Public and products liability. Limit of indemnity $5,000,000 any one
occurrence and in the aggregate for products. Excess $2,500 each and every
claim.

Section F - Conditions precedent. The Insured shall not admit liability, settle,
or offer to settle any claim without the prior written consent of the Insurer.
Breach of this condition entitles the Insurer to decline the claim in whole.

Section H - Exclusions. This policy does not cover any liability for taxation,
duties, levies or charges of any kind, nor any professional fee incurred in
connection with a taxation matter, however arising.

# Larkin Fabrication Ltd - assessment closed
From: Meridian Revenue Authority, Assessments Branch
To: Halloran & Reese, agents. Our reference AB/4471/02.

We confirm that the assessment referenced above has been finalised and that no
further correspondence is expected on it. The 14 reconciling items raised at the
interim stage were resolved on the papers, 11 of them without adjustment, and
the remaining 3 by agreement at a net figure of $4,180. The file has been marked
closed at this end.

Nothing in this letter indicates the Authority's view on any other period. Where
an agent wishes to raise a matter relating to a different period, the ordinary
channels remain available and the published response time of 28 working days
applies.

We remind agents that correspondence sent to this Branch should carry the client
reference in the subject line. Approximately 340 items were delayed last quarter
because the reference appeared in the body of the letter only, and sorting at
this office is done on the subject line alone.

No action is required of you in respect of this letter.

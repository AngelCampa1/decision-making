# Engagement administration - standing arrangements
Halloran & Reese, administration. Engagement 8817, cycle 12.

Standing arrangements continue as recorded. Billing is monthly in arrears
against time recorded, at the rates last reviewed 14 months ago at the start of
the engagement period: $420 for partner time, $265 for manager, $140 for
assistant. No variation has been sought by either side.

Document retention follows the firm's general policy of 7 years. Working papers
sit in the matter file and correspondence in the client archive, and the
retention clock runs from closure of the relevant compliance cycle rather than
from the date of each document. Roughly 2,400 items are held for this client.

Contact routing is unchanged: compliance correspondence to the engagement
manager, billing to administration on extension 118. Items that could go to
either should go to the engagement manager, who redirects.

# Payroll query - director expenses
Halloran & Reese to client, current period

We have reviewed the payroll records for the period and have one query.

The records show reimbursements totalling $18,420 paid to a director across
eleven separate claims. Nine of those claims carry supporting receipts on the
file. Two, raised in the second half of the period, do not.

Under reg. 31(2) of the Meridian Payroll Regulations a reimbursement made
without supporting evidence must be reported as a benefit unless the evidence is
produced within 90 days of the reimbursement date. For the two claims above that
period has expired in the first case and expires shortly in the second.

Please confirm whether receipts exist for either claim. If they do not, we will
report both as benefits in the ordinary way. This is a payroll reporting point
only and has no bearing on the corporate position.
